# Donate

Draggy is free. There's no subscription, no account and no telemetry. I'm lucki74 and I build it on my own.

If it saves you time or money, you can buy me a coffee on [ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/lucki74). One-time tips and monthly support both help a lot.

Monthly support goes toward the Apple Developer Program ($99 a year), so I can sign Draggy for macOS and Mac users get a proper install.

Starring the repo also helps, since it makes Draggy easier for other people to find.
