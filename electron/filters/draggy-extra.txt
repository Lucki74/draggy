! Title: Draggy supplemental
! Description: First-party advertising and telemetry endpoints that the
!   general-purpose lists leave alone for compatibility, but which carry no
!   user-visible function.
!
! Why this file exists
! --------------------
! EasyList, uBlock Origin and AdGuard are written to be safe on every site on
! the web, which means they do not blanket-block a platform's own domains: one
! over-broad rule against facebook.com or googlevideo.com breaks logging in or
! playing a video for millions of people, and the lists rightly refuse that
! trade.
!
! The endpoints below are the subset where that caution costs nothing. Each is
! an advertising API, a measurement pixel, or an operating-system telemetry
! sink. None of them renders anything, carries a login, or delivers media, so
! blocking them removes tracking without removing behaviour.
!
! Anything load-bearing is deliberately NOT here, and the list at the bottom
! of this file says which and why, so nobody adds them later by mistake.

! --- Deliberately absent ----------------------------------------------------
! These appear in ad-blocker scoring tests and would raise the score, and are
! left alone because each one carries something the user asked for:
!
!   connect.facebook.net      the SDK behind "log in with Facebook"
!   graph.facebook.com        the same SDK's API
!   graph.instagram.com       Instagram itself
!   i.instagram.com           Instagram's own images
!   s.youtube.com             YouTube playback
!   redirector.googlevideo.com  YouTube's video CDN
!   g.jwpsrv.com              JW Player, a video player
!   ssl.p.jwpcdn.com          the same player's assets
!   tagmanager.google.com     often loads a site's own functionality
!   widgets.pinterest.com     Pinterest embeds
!   widget.intercom.io        the support chat a site offers
!   js.driftt.com             the same
!   clientstream.launchdarkly.com  feature flags; blocking changes behaviour
!   cdn.dynamicyield.com      personalisation that can take layout with it
