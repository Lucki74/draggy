# Project plan

## 1. Define

- **Goal:** what done looks like, measurably ("new website live with 12 pages and a contact form by 1 June").
- **Scope:** what is included and explicitly excluded.
- **Constraints:** deadline, budget, people available, fixed dates.
- **Stakeholders:** who decides, who does the work, who must be kept informed.

## 2. Break down

1. Split the work into 3 to 7 phases or milestones, each with a clear deliverable.
2. Split each into tasks small enough to estimate: roughly half a day to five days each.
3. For each task: owner (or role), estimate, and what it depends on.
4. Find the critical path: the chain of dependent tasks that sets the end date. Point it out.
5. Add buffer: about 15 to 25% on estimates for uncertain work, and a buffer before the deadline.

## 3. Risks

List the top 5 risks with likelihood, impact and a mitigation or early warning sign for each.

## 4. Communicate

Suggest a simple rhythm: weekly check-in, who updates the plan, how decisions and changes are recorded.

## Output

Milestones with dates, then the task list, then risks. Offer to save:
- an .xlsx with create_file: columns ID, Phase, Task, Owner, Estimate (days), Depends on, Start, End, Status;
- and/or a .docx or .pdf project brief with goal, scope, milestones and risks.

Dates as YYYY-MM-DD in the spreadsheet. Skip weekends when computing dates unless told otherwise, and say so.
