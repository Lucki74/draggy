Four steps take you from nothing to a first reply.

1. [Install Draggy](doc:installation) and start it.
2. Setup picks a model that fits your hardware and asks you to confirm it. The download starts when you continue. [Choosing a model](doc:models) explains the pick.
3. Write in the composer on the Chat side and send it. The reply is made on this computer.
4. To work on files, switch to Code with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>X</kbd> and give it a [project](doc:projects) folder. It asks before commands, moves and deletes, as [Permissions](doc:permissions) describes.

[Chat and Code](doc:chat-and-code) covers the two sides in full.
