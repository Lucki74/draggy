## Theme

`Settings → General → Appearance` sets the theme to light, dark, or match the system.

## Accent color

The accent colors buttons, the progress bar, switches, links, selection and focus rings. There are seven: vermilion (the default), blue, orange, green, pink, sky blue and yellow.

## Text size

The same group sets the text size, from small to large.
