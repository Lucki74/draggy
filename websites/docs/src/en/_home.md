Draggy runs [GGUF](doc:glossary#gguf) models with an [embedded engine](doc:glossary#engine) ([llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggerganov/llama.cpp)), using open weights from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co). There is no account to make. Some requests go out without you asking: update checks, and ad-block lists unless you switch ad blocking off. Conversations stay on your computer unless you add a [cloud provider](doc:glossary#provider).
