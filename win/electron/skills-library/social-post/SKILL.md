# Social posts

## Platform habits

- **LinkedIn:** up to about 1,300 characters before "see more" matters; the first two lines must earn the click. Short lines, white space, a personal angle, a question or takeaway at the end, 3 to 5 hashtags at most.
- **X / Twitter:** 280 characters per post. For more, write a thread: the first post stands alone and promises what follows; number the posts (1/6). One or two hashtags or none.
- **Bluesky / Mastodon:** 300 and 500 characters. Plainer, less promotional tone; hashtags help discovery on Mastodon (camel case for accessibility, like #OpenSource).
- **Instagram:** the caption supports the image; first line is the hook, then value, then a call to action, hashtags grouped at the end (up to 10 relevant ones).
- **Facebook:** conversational, short, a question invites comments.

## Writing

1. Ask for or infer: platform, goal (awareness, clicks, discussion, hiring, announcement), audience, and any link or handle to include.
2. Lead with the most interesting part: the result, the number, the surprise, the lesson.
3. One message per post. Cut anything that does not serve it.
4. Use emoji only if the user's style or the brand does, and never more than a few.
5. Count characters for length-limited platforms and stay under the limit, including links (a link counts as 23 characters on X).

## Output

Give 2 or 3 variants, each labelled with its angle (for example "story", "data-led", "question"), with the character count for X, Bluesky and Mastodon. For a thread, show every post numbered.
