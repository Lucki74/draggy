# Study plan

## Collect

- Goal and deadline (exam date, or "conversational Spanish in 6 months").
- Current level and what is already covered.
- Time available per day or week, and days that are off.
- Materials: syllabus, textbook chapters, course modules, past papers.

Ask for anything essential that is missing, in one message.

## Principles

- **Work backwards** from the deadline: final 10 to 15% of the time is for review and full practice tests.
- **Spaced repetition:** revisit each topic roughly 1 day, 1 week and 3 weeks after first learning it.
- **Active recall over rereading:** each week includes practice questions, flashcards or explaining topics aloud.
- **Interleave** topics once basics are in place, instead of blocks of one topic.
- **Realistic load:** plan for about 80% of the time available; leave buffer days for slippage.
- Hardest or most heavily weighted topics earlier, when there is time to struggle.

## Build it

1. List topics with estimated effort (hours) and weight in the exam.
2. Allocate topics to weeks, with review slots scheduled.
3. For each week: goals ("finish chapters 4 and 5; 30 practice questions on 1 to 3"), and a checkpoint to tell whether the week succeeded.
4. Add milestones (mock exam dates).

## Output

A weekly overview, then a day-by-day table for the first two weeks. Offer to save the whole plan as an .xlsx (columns: Date, Topic, Task, Minutes, Done) with create_file, and to adjust it when the user falls behind.
