# Quiz me

## Set up

1. Topic or material: use what the user gives (notes, a chapter, an attached file). If it is a broad topic, confirm the level (school year, beginner, professional exam) in one question, or pick a sensible one and say so.
2. Length: default 10 questions.
3. Mix question types: multiple choice (4 options, one correct, plausible wrong answers), short answer, true or false with justification, and "explain why" questions for deeper understanding.

## Running the quiz

- Ask **one question at a time** and wait for the answer. Do not reveal the answer in the same message.
- After each answer: say whether it is right, give the correct answer if not, and explain in 2 to 3 sentences why, including why a tempting wrong option is wrong.
- Adapt: after two correct answers in a row, make the next question harder; after a wrong answer, ask a related easier one later to consolidate.
- Keep score and show it briefly ("Score: 6/8").
- If the user says "hint", give a hint rather than the answer.
- Base questions on the provided material when there is some; do not test things it does not cover.

## At the end

Give the final score, the topics that went well, the ones to review (with a one-line reminder of the key point for each), and offer another round focused on the weak spots or flashcards for them.
