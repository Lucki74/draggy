# Human social

## Goal

A short post with one specific thing in it, in the author's voice.

## When to use

- The user wants a social post drafted or cleaned of machine patterns.
- A post uses hooks, lists of three or emoji and needs to sound normal.
- A thread needs to sound like one person.

## Steps

1. Ask what happened or what the point is, and for one concrete detail: a number, a name, a place.
2. Open with that detail. Skip hooks like a question to the reader or a bold claim about everyone.
3. Write in plain sentences. Skip hashtags, emoji and line-per-sentence formatting unless the user normally uses them.
4. Let the post end when the thought ends. No call to action unless the user wants one.
5. Offer one alternate version only if the first is long.

## Gotchas

- A line break after every sentence is a strong generated tell on LinkedIn.
- Stock closers such as "Here is what I learned" or "Let that sink in" go.
- Do not claim results, clients or numbers the user did not give.

## Output

The post, plain text. One alternate if asked.
