Brindle is a task queue for Python. It runs background jobs on a pool of workers and retries the ones that fail.

Brindle is maintained by Tomasz Okafor-Reyes, who works at Marlow Systems. The current series is 4.x and the latest release is 4.6.

Docs: [retries in 4.x](https://docs.brindle.example/4.x/retries), [changelog](https://docs.brindle.example/changelog).
