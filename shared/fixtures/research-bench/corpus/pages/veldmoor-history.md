Veldmoor was founded in 1788 as a salt-works settlement called Saltry. It was renamed Veldmoor in 1851.

An earlier version of this page gave 1849 for the renaming. A review of the council archive in 2024 showed that the council voted in 1850 and the new name took effect in 1851.
