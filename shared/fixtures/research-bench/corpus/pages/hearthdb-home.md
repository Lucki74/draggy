Hearthdb is an embedded key-value store written in Go. It is made by Marlow Systems.

Hearthdb stores data in sorted levels and merges them in the background. Read about [compaction](https://hearthdb.example/docs/compaction) and the [release notes](https://hearthdb.example/releases).
