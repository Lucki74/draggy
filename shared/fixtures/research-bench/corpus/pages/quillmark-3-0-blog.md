Today we are releasing Quillmark 3.0.

**A new license.** Quillmark moves from MIT to MPL-2.0. The change was requested by our sponsor, Lumen & Oar Ltd, which ships Quillmark inside a commercial product and wanted changes to the library to flow back to the project.

**Speed.** On the atlas corpus, a 1.2 MB collection of mixed documents, 3.0 renders 41% faster than 2.9.

**Requirements.** The minimum supported Rust version is 1.74.

Thanks to everyone who tested the release candidates. Ines Valkonen, maintainer
