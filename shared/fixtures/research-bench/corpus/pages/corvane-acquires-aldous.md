Halvern, 3 February 2026. Corvane Robotics has acquired Aldous Instruments, the maker of the AI-90 pressure sensor, for EUR 31 million.

Aldous will keep operating under its own name from its existing site. Mikael Soderqvist, co-founder of Corvane, said the sensor line fits the Tern drones well.
