Fenwright Labs. Lead author: Dr. Anneliese Prokop, with Daichi Moriyama.

We measure write stalls during compaction in three embedded stores, including Hearthdb 2.1 with its default 32 MiB threshold. For Hearthdb the 99th-percentile stall was 212 ms.
