Lumen & Oar Ltd is a typesetting company. It was founded in 2009 in Veldmoor by Beatrix Hollin, and it has 38 employees.

Our main product is Inkwell, a desktop app for laying out books and reports. We have sponsored the open-source Markdown renderer Quillmark since 2023.

Visit us at the old salt warehouse on Harbour Row in Veldmoor.
