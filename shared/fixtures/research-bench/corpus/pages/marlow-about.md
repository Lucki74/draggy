Marlow Systems builds database software. The company was founded in 2014 by Odalys Brandt and is headquartered in Kessing Bay.

Our best-known product is Hearthdb, an embedded key-value store written in Go. About 90 people work at Marlow, among them Tomasz Okafor-Reyes, maintainer of the Python task queue Brindle.
