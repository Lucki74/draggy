quillmark-lite is an independent fork of Quillmark by Dario Estrada. It is released under the MIT license and tracks Quillmark 2.9.2, the last MIT release of the original project.

The fork needs Rust 1.65. It drops several extensions, and in the author's own tests it renders 55% faster than Quillmark 2.9 on small inputs.

quillmark-lite is not affiliated with the Quillmark project or its sponsor.
